A Complete Disproof of the Circuit Odd-Cover Conjecture (Conjecture 5.2)

The circuit odd-cover conjecture asks whether every Eulerian simple binary matroid \(M\) satisfies \(c_2(M)\leq a(M)\)—equivalently, whether \(M\) can be odd-covered by no more ambient circuits than its arboricity. This note constructs a rank-8, 24-element example with \(a(M)=3\) and \(c_2(M)=4\), giving an explicit counterexample.
